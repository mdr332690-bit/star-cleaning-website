The homepage title is **Cleaning Services in Kuwait | Star Cleaning**. Its description is **Star Cleaning offers home, office, villa, sofa and carpet cleaning, plus deep cleaning in Kuwait. Explore our services and prepare a cleaning enquiry.** English and Arabic copy naturally covers home cleaning, office cleaning, villa cleaning, sofa and carpet cleaning, and deep cleaning in Kuwait. No keyword meta tag, hidden keyword text or unsupported business claims were added.

English remains the server-rendered language at `/`. The language control continues to provide Arabic content and RTL layouts on that same URL, remembers the preference when storage is available and updates the browser title and descriptive social metadata. This does not create separately indexable Arabic pages. Hreflang annotations were deliberately omitted because there are no distinct language URLs; the sitemap likewise does not list fragments or nonexistent service pages.

The business schema includes only verified information and Kuwait-wide coverage. It omits a street address, coordinates, phone number, opening hours, prices and reviews because those details have not been supplied. Country-wide service coverage is not a substitute for a verified business address. The graph describes the business, but address-dependent Google Local Business rich-result eligibility cannot be established from the supplied information. The existing experience and client counts were retained without adding new claims, and testimonials remain explicitly marked as awaiting verified reviews.

The existing Google Search Console verification tag was preserved. After deployment, submit `https://star-cleaning-website.netlify.app/sitemap.xml` in the site's Search Console property and inspect the homepage URL to request indexing. Account verification, sitemap submission, Google Business Profile management and Google indexing were not performed or claimed. If the primary domain changes, update `site.url`, robots.txt and sitemap.xml together.

Validation passed with `pnpm run typecheck`, formatting checks and whitespace checks. HTTP checks confirmed the rendered homepage metadata, parseable JSON-LD, working crawl files, responsive WebP assets, locally served fonts and a proper missing-page response. Chromium checks covered English and Arabic at 320, 375, 760, 1024 and 1440 pixels without horizontal overflow; language persistence and blocked-storage behavior; fragment targets; service expansion and dialogs; enquiry preparation; video loading only after opening its dialog; and navigation from the 404 page. No browser page errors were observed. These were development-environment checks; production builds are validated by Netlify, and production Core Web Vitals and indexing should be monitored after deployment.
