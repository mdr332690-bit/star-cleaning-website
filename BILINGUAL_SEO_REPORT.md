# Star Cleaning Service Kuwait: bilingual SEO report

Reviewed on 22 November 2026. Production origin: https://star-cleaning-website.netlify.app/.

The website was optimized for English and Arabic cleaning enquiries in Kuwait while preserving its forest green, ivory and brass design, typography, verified phone number, enquiry forms, WhatsApp editor and existing service routes. Changes were prepared for review. No production deployment or automatic merge was performed.

The actual source and representative published pages were inspected first. The baseline already included 12 language-specific URLs, server-rendered SEO metadata, reciprocal language links, a bilingual sitemap, service content and structured data. This work refined that foundation rather than introducing duplicate pages for keywords. The changes described here supersede older audit reports where their findings differ.

## 5. Remaining SEO issues and external work

1. **Review and publication:** the changes require review and an approved publication before Google can crawl the new wording. Production build validation is handled separately; no build command or production deployment was initiated in this run.
2. **Area availability:** coverage in the seven named locations remains unverified. Confirm actual service areas with the business before making stronger local claims or changing structured-data coverage.
3. **Business details and local enhancements:** a verified physical address, opening hours and other unpublished business details were not supplied. They were not fabricated. Schema.org LocalBusiness markup alone does not establish eligibility for Google's LocalBusiness rich results; missing required verified information can limit eligibility.
4. **Google Business Profile and reviews:** verified profile/review destinations remain unset, so their links remain hidden. Supply only approved real destinations and maintain accurate business details and service areas. Testimonials remain explicitly awaiting verified reviews.
5. **Search Console access:** ownership, sitemap submission history, actual index coverage and Google's chosen canonicals were not inspected through an authenticated account. The existing verification tag was preserved, but its presence does not prove verified ownership.
6. **Performance:** production mobile PageSpeed and field Core Web Vitals need checking after an approved publication. Existing Ads and Tag Manager scripts were preserved and remain a possible loading-cost review area. No Lighthouse score, ranking gain or field performance result was invented or carried forward from older reports as a current measurement.
